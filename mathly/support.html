Frequently asked questions

Which languages does Mathly speak?

English, German and Romanian. Change the language with the EN / DE / RO buttons on the helper screen or the home screen.

How do I change my helper?

Tap "Change helper" on the home screen and pick any of the 16 animal friends.

Where are my scores saved?

On your device only. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.

Does Mathly need internet?

No. It works completely offline.

There are no sounds.

Check the sound button on the home screen and your phone's media volume and silent mode.

I found a mistake or a bug.

Please email us with your phone model, Android version and a short description. A screenshot helps.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No. Mathly is a one-time purchase with no ads and no in-app purchases.
